# Educational evidence and limitations

The computer-science example is an independently authored broad learning map. The text, exercises, fine-grained prerequisite judgments and connection reasons are educational design. They are not a university-approved syllabus, a full disciplinary encyclopedia or a certified doctoral completion plan.

Public references used to orient the example:

- [ACM / IEEE-CS / AAAI CS2023](https://csed.acm.org/): breadth across computer-science knowledge areas. The example does not reproduce its curriculum or claim formal compliance.
- [Stanford CS foundations and breadth](https://www.cs.stanford.edu/phd-program-foundation-and-breadth-requirements): illustrates the distinction between foundations, breadth and specialization. Local deadlines, course counts and waivers are not copied into the generic example.
- [CMU doctoral computer-science overview](https://www.csd.cmu.edu/academics/doctoral/phd-in-computer-science): illustrates original research, disciplinary breadth, communication and dissertation work. Its duration and requirements are not treated as universal.
- [MIT EECS doctoral milestones](https://www.eecs.mit.edu/academics/graduate-programs/materials-and-forms-for-graduate-students/phd-academic-progress-i/): another example of institution-specific milestone organization; not imposed on the tree.

The example supports different research evidence: proofs, systems measurements, data experiments, human-participant studies and other relevant analyses. It does not force every doctoral learner into AI, systems implementation, a separate master's degree, a fixed duration or a publication quota.

Graph validation checks references, structural consistency and connectivity. It cannot prove the academic accuracy of all edges or the sufficiency of the study plan. Verify specialized claims against appropriate primary texts, courses or methods. Verify formal degree requirements against the actual institution and cohort. Never claim full-text review based only on a title, abstract or overview.
